A bit about me

Born in New York.
Built in Toronto.

I'm a television producer and documentary director with nearly a decade of experience across broadcast, streaming, and independent film. My credits include series for Apple TV+, Prime Video, and The Weather Channel, and my debut feature documentary is headed to festivals in 2026.

Whether I'm embedded with Search and Rescue crews on Vancouver Island or documenting a dying oyster industry in PEI, I go wherever the story demands, and I stay until I get it right.

I
New York City
Where it started. Staten Island born, Queens raised, shaped by the energy, the culture, and a relentless curiosity that's never fully switched off.
II
Fort Lauderdale
A childhood chapter that widened the lens. South Florida taught me to stay present and find joy in unlikely places, a lesson that's quietly shaped how I approach every project.
III
Toronto
Where I became a professional, built lasting friendships, and learned that the richest stories come from the intersections of cultures, something Toronto has in abundance.
IV
What's Next
A debut feature documentary premiering in 2026, continued series work, and a growing slate of projects that push further into character-driven storytelling across film and television.
